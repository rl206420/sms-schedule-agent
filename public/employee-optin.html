MR GT Employee Shift Schedule Line - Enrollment

Business: MR GT LLC

How to enroll:

1. Text START to (906) 767-6750 from your mobile phone.
2. You will receive a confirmation message: "You're opted in to MR GT Scheduling. Text your availability and shift schedule to this number. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
3. From then on, text your availability and shift information to this number whenever needed. Management uses this to build the work schedule.

Consent: By texting START (or any message) to (906) 767-6750, you consent to receive text messages from MR GT related to shift scheduling. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.

Enrollment is limited to current MR GT employees who have been informed of this line by management.
