Terms and Conditions - Employee Shift Schedule Line

Last updated: 2026

This SMS line is used by MR GT employees to submit their availability and shift schedule information for internal scheduling purposes.

Participation

Participation is limited to current MR GT employees. Employees are informed directly by management that they may text their availability and shift schedule information to this number.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on scheduling needs. Typically a few messages per week per employee.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on the employee's mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out

Employees may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from this number. Reply HELP for assistance.

Contact
