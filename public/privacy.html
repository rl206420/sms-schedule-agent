This is an internal text messaging line used by MR GT employees to submit their availability and shift schedule information, which is used to build the work schedule.

Information We Collect

When an employee texts (906) 767-6750, we collect their mobile phone number and the content of their message (availability and shift details) in order to build and manage the work schedule.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to build employee work schedules internally. We do not use employee mobile numbers for marketing or any purpose unrelated to scheduling.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not sell, rent, or share employee mobile phone numbers or message content with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes, at any time, for any reason.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on scheduling needs. Typically a few messages per week per employee.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on the employee's mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out

Employees may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from this number. Reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to MR GT LLC management.
