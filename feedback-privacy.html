Privacy Policy - Customer Feedback SMS Line

Last updated: 2026

This policy covers our SMS-based customer feedback line, which customers use to share favorite product flavors, compliments, and complaints.

Information We Collect

When you text this number, we collect your mobile phone number and the content of your message in order to respond to you and, where relevant, follow up on your feedback.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information only to respond to your message, track feedback trends, and follow up on complaints internally. We do not use your mobile number for unrelated marketing without separate consent.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or message content with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes, at any time, for any reason.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with this line. Typically you will receive one reply per message you send.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from this number. Reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to our team through this number or our other listed contact channels.
